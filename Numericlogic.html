Numerical Reasoning Assessment

Speed, Distance & Time • Percentages

1. A vehicle travels 96 km at 48 km/h and then another 132 km at 66 km/h. What is the average speed for the entire journey?

2. A train is scheduled to cover 270 km in 3 hours. After travelling for 1 hour at 75 km/h, what average speed must it maintain for the remaining journey to arrive exactly on time?

3. Two cyclists start from the same point and travel in opposite directions. Cyclist A travels at 24 km/h and Cyclist B at 32 km/h. After how long will they be 126 km apart?

4. A driver increases his speed from 72 km/h to 90 km/h. For a journey that originally took 2 hours 30 minutes, how much travelling time is saved if the distance remains unchanged?

5. An electronic device has a marked price of $480. A 15% discount is applied, followed by an additional 10% discount on the reduced price. What is the final price?

6. A product's price is increased by 20%. The new price is then reduced by 20%. If the original price was $750, what is the final price?

7. In an assessment, a candidate answers 72% of the first 200 questions correctly and 84% of the next 100 questions correctly. What percentage of all 300 questions did the candidate answer correctly?

8. After receiving a 12% discount, a customer pays $616 for an item. What was the item's original price?

Please answer all 8 questions before submitting.
